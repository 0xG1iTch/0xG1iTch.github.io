# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-selected Astro static generation, TypeScript, React only for interactive islands, Tailwind CSS, Markdown/MDX, and typed Astro Content Collections. Compatible with future GitHub Pages deployment.

## Product Purpose
A unified AI engineering portfolio, technical publication, and digital journal. Published work demonstrates expertise; projects appear as detailed technical stories.

## Users
Inferred from the brief: engineers reading technical material, potential collaborators, and people evaluating the author's engineering work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Home, Writing, About, and article routes. One feed supports articles, projects, experiments, and notes, with lightweight filters. Long-form pages support code, images, diagrams, tables, metadata, SEO, accessible controls, and mobile layouts. No backend, database, authentication, or CMS. Minimal dependencies and JavaScript.

## Brand Commitments
Swiss / International Typographic style, precise grids, magazine composition, clear typography, generous whitespace, and subtle interaction. Dark palette: background #141819, text #F1F0EA, borders #3B4343, accent #B6C7BB. Both themes must use only these four literal colors: no additional green shades or color blends. Light mode uses warm white, charcoal, and slate for text and links. The hero CTA is a plain text link without a rectangle background. Keep the site focused on sharing knowledge and demonstrating understanding through published work. The homepage starts with a concise production-AI statement and one primary CTA, followed by In focus, up to four latest posts, an explicit full-archive link, and topic navigation. Keep the utility footer.

## Product Principles
- The portfolio is the blog; the blog is the portfolio.
- Content is separate from presentation.
- Deliver reading content as static HTML.
- Make expertise visible through useful technical work and shared knowledge.
- Prefer concise introductions and practical navigation to personal-brand slogans.
